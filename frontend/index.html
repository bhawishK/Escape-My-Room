Checking VR support…

The room fills the screen. Tap the box to select it; it is outlined in pink and only you see the selection. With Walk with steps on, keep Hold to move pressed and walk: the box comes with you, step by step, and stays where you let go. Turn with phone looks around as you turn. Walk with steps moves the view a step at a time as you walk, roughly; it counts steps rather than measuring where you are. Gaze cursor points at objects with your eyes or head through the front camera, after a short calibration. Measure height sets the view to how high the phone is, measured once from the back camera with an AI depth model; it downloads 76 MB the first time.

iPhone 16 Chrome shows the room full screen and turns it with the phone. Meta Quest 3 uses the immersive VR view.
