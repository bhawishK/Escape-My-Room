Checking VR support…

The room fills the screen and follows you. It turns as the phone turns and, roughly, moves a step at a time as you walk.

  • Tap the box to select it. It’s outlined in pink, and only you see the selection.
  • Press and hold to pick it up. It stays in front of you as you walk and turn.
  • Let go to drop it on the floor. Tap anywhere else to deselect.
  • Gaze cursor points at objects with your eyes or head through the front camera, after a short calibration.
  • Measure height measures once how high the phone is, from the back camera. It downloads 76 MB the first time.
