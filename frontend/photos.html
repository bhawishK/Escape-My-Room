            Room video

            Film the room once; the GPU desktop turns the video into a splat room.

            • Film at 1080p, with HDR off (Settings → Camera → Record Video).
            • Walk slowly round the edge of the room, facing across it.
            • Film each end of the room both ways: walk there, turn round, and walk back. The phone finds its place in the room from these views.
            • Keep it under 5 minutes.
