Furniture

    Choose a piece, then press on the room map where it goes and drag the way it faces.

    In VR

    • Voice: press Start voice and allow the microphone before Enter VR; the call carries on inside VR. On a controller, B or Y mutes and unmutes.
    • Controllers: A or X lines the room up; squeeze the grip on an object to move it.
    • Hands: pinch to line the room up; pinch on an object, or look at it and pinch, to move it.
    • Point with the index finger to show something; hold an open palm to pause in a calm view.
    • Dollhouse: pinch with both hands and pull them together to shrink the room, apart to grow it back; let go near life size and it lines up with the real room. While it is small, pinch on nothing with one hand to move and turn it. The phone user is the small figure inside.
    • Furnishing the dollhouse: while it is small, the catalogue floats above your left hand. Pinch a piece with your right hand, carry it over the dollhouse and let go where it goes; turn your hand to turn it. Pinch a placed piece to move it, or toss it.
    • Look at an object for a moment to start a topic about it; nod for yes, shake for no.
