In VR

  • Voice: press Start voice and allow the microphone before Enter VR; the call carries on inside VR. On a controller, B or Y mutes and unmutes.
  • Controllers: A or X lines the room up; squeeze the grip on an object to move it.
  • Hands: pinch to line the room up; pinch on an object, or look at it and pinch, to move it.
  • Point with the index finger to show something; hold an open palm to pause in a calm view.
  • Look at an object for a moment to start a topic about it; nod for yes, shake for no.
