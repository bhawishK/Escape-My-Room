    In VR

    • Voice: press Start voice and allow the microphone before Enter VR; the call carries on inside VR. Hold up a fist for a moment to mute, and again to unmute; on a controller, B or Y does the same.
    • Controllers: A or X lines the room up; squeeze the grip on an object to move it.
    • Hands: pinch to line the room up; pinch on an object to move it; in the dollhouse, looking at it and pinching works too.
    • Point with the index finger to show something.
    • Dollhouse: pinch with both hands and pull them together to shrink the room, apart to grow it back; let go near life size and it lines up with the real room. While it is small, pinch on nothing with one hand to move and turn it. The phone user is the small figure inside.
    • Wrist menu: open your left hand, palm up, to show the menu above it; close it into a fist to hide it. Pinch the slider along its top with your right hand and slide, or pinch a stop, to go between Objects, Make, Drill, Edit and Exit. On Drill, pinch an object to choose it and pinch Prepare; when it is ready, pinch Start, Stop to end, and Call off to take it away. It is the same as the Drill card here.
    • Objects: pinch a piece, carry it into the room or the dollhouse and let go where it goes; turn your hand to turn it. Pinch a placed piece to move it, or toss it.
    • Make: pinch and hold Hold to speak, say the piece, let go, then pinch Make it. Its progress shows on the menu, and the new piece joins Objects.
    • Edit: tap a placed piece (a quick pinch and let go) to choose it; the menu turns to Edit. Move takes hold of it. After Resize, pinch with both hands and pull them apart to grow it, together to shrink it, from a quarter to three times its size; let go to finish.
    • Exit: pinch Exit VR to leave VR.
    • Look at an object for a moment to start a topic about it; nod for yes, shake for no.
